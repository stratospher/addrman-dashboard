Bitcoin P2P — analysis dashboards

Local, browser-only tools for inspecting node state. Nothing is uploaded — every file you open is parsed in this page and never leaves the machine.

Available

Addrman health
Feed a getrawaddrman dump and see the timestamp distribution across the new and tried tables — last-refresh histogram, age bands, and the terrible-by-horizon share.
Relay health
Feed grepped Received addr: lines and see volume and packing over time, the gossip / mid / bulk channel split, rate-limit intensity and concentration, and any unsolicited bulk pushes.

Planned

Churn — flushed out vs entering
Needs two dumps taken apart in time; diffs eviction against insertion.
ASN vs netgroup bucketing
Requires an asmap file — ASN is not present in a getrawaddrman dump.
Network mix, nRefCount, nAttempts
Network mix and nRefCount come from a getrawaddrman dump; nAttempts and m_last_success are absent there but are serialized in peers.dat, which also carries nKey — enough to evaluate the full IsTerrible() and to recompute bucketing under an asmap.
Sustained-spam simulator
Models what a continuous injection rate does to occupancy and eviction.

Getting a dump

  • bitcoin-cli getrawaddrman > addrman.json
  • Hourly cron snapshots are .json.zst — run zstd -d first; the browser can read plain .json and .json.gz.
